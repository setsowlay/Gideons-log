Captain’s LogEntry I

The First Entry

This is the first entry into the expedition captain's expedition log of expedition #4. I am Gideon Stonetoes and I am leading an expedition of 7 dwarves into the deep dark. My family has been leading these expeditions for the last 3 generations with me being the 4th. I have sired my sons, left a pile of gold for my family, and am now leaving to fulfill my destiny, and that of my lineage.

The King is sending us on this mission because he cares greatly about exploring the worlds surrounding the Dwarven people. These logs and missives sent back to the King will chronicle our journey. All of us leaving on this journey know that we may not be able to return home, but we do this for the good of the Kingdom, for glory, for our names, and also just because it’s badass.

I’m not much of a writer so most of these will be short, but hopefully we see enough things of interest that it won’t matter. My understanding is that these will be made public in the royal archives once the King and his team of scholars have had a chance to strike things out and hopefully make me sound a bit smarter than I am.

Missive to the High KingEntry II

The First Missive

High King Brandolin,

I send to you the first missive of the fourth grand expedition into the deep roads for the glory of the Kingdom. While the journey started with pounding in our heads and bubbles in our guts after the grand ceremony you held for us, my team is well trained and ready, we are equipped with the greatest tools and weapons the Royal Artificers can offer.

We hope to discover many things, acquire many treasures, and secure much glory not just for ourselves, but for the Dwarven Kingdom. I will send you regular missives, often along with those of the others on my team with discoveries by our scholar, our wizard, or our expert miner. We will also send any objects or samples back with one of the carter teams that will be travelling along the tracks.

My family has been leading these expeditions for 4 generations now. I hope to continue to bring back glory to my family name. I set out on this expedition with thoughts of every dwarf in the kingdom held in my heart.

— Gidon Stonetoes

Missive to the High KingEntry III

Good Time Made

My King,

We have been making good time. We are already at the end of the mining level, about to enter the Deep Dark. While our journey has barely started, we are all excited and anxious to see what we will discover in the deepest recesses of the ancient caves. Hopefully the cart tracks will carry us far, but if we must walk then we will.

Overall the journey has gone well so far. It is clear our team is made up of some of the greatest that the Kingdom has to offer. Even if some of them are endlessly annoying. Beldrun has been writing 15 letters everyday, one to each of his kids and another to his wife. I believe we will run out of parchment within the week. Babraen and Braggouc are both mighty warriors, but they seem to be unable to stop talking about wild stories about the deep dark even into the night. I think if we don’t come across any giant beasts or ancient warriors they will be sorely disappointed. Add to this the endless mumbling of our wizard Dhussor, who seems to incessantly talk to himself about nothing at all, there is always a good deal of noise.

Despite the struggles so far, I still persist in leading this expedition to great discoveries and gathering of glory for the Kingdom. We will delve deeper than any Dwarf has delved before!

Captain’s LogEntry IV — The Bottomless Pit

A Big Hole

Today we found a big hole. The tracks travel right next to it, so we decided to make camp right near it. We weren’t sure how deep the hole went, so our Engineer experimented:

  • Urinating down the hole - nothing happened, except it was kind of scary to be that close to the hole with your ….thing…. out
  • Dropping a rock down the hole - no sound after a few minutes
  • Dropping a bigger rock down the hole - no sound after a few minutes
  • Dropping a lantern down the hole - no sound made, light eventually disappears
  • Our miner for some reason felt dedicated to mining some steps out, he got down pretty far before he got bored and decided to stop
  • Dropping a lit firedust barrel down the hole - eventually it exploded, but nothing really happened.

Our scientific conclusions from this experiment are that the hole is really deep.
